Where SRA-only submissions record their papers¶

Question: for a sequencing submission with no GEO Series, where is its paper recorded, and how often? Does seqforge's demo dataset, PRJNA1027859 (SRA study SRP502277), have one?

All checks ran on 2026-10-14 against live NCBI E-utilities, Europe PMC and ENA, with metadata only. Each claim is tagged measured (counted from a query), read (seen in a record or schema) or inferred.

Answer¶

  • The submitter-side records rarely carry a paper. Across GEO-less sequencing BioProjects, about 7% have a PubMed or DOI link anywhere in NCBI's structured metadata. GEO-linked BioProjects have one far more often (77%).
  • The paper usually exists anyway. About half of the same projects are cited by accession in Europe PMC's literature index, and the earliest citing article is usually the paper that produced the data.
  • The NCBI routes are not independent. A BioProject Publication element shows up in the bioproject→pubmed link, the sra→pubmed link and the SRA STUDY_LINKS together. PubMed's own databank field ([si]) is a separate route, which no Entrez link exposes.
  • The demo dataset has a paper: Cell Genomics 2024, PMID 39637862 (PMC11701262). Only the publication side records it, in PubMed's databank field and in Europe PMC. The BioProject, the SRA study and every Entrez link carry nothing.

BioProject esummary no publication field read
elink bioproject→pubmed no link set read

SRA STUDY XML no STUDY_LINKS element read

The paper is "Adult single-nucleus neuronal transcriptomes of insulin signaling mutants reveal regulators of behavior and learning", Cell Genomics 2024, doi:10.1016/j.xgen.2024.100720. Its title differs from the BioProject title, so a title search does not find it (read).

Census: Entrez link counts¶

BioProject search filters, registration dates 2019-01-01 to 2022-12-31 (measured):

Set BioProjects With a bioproject→pubmed link
Has SRA data, no GEO link 177,131 8,528 (4.8%)
Same, project data type "raw sequence reads" 92,540 2,307 (2.5%)
Has SRA data and a GEO link 63,075 48,494 (77%)

Query: bioproject_sra[filter] AND 2019/01/01:2022/12/31[DATE], combined with bioproject_gds[filter], "raw sequence reads"[DTPE] and bioproject_pubmed[filter]. The date window leaves at least three years for a paper to appear.

Sample: every route on 200 projects¶

Method: all 92,540 UIDs from the "raw sequence reads, no GEO" set above, then a random sample of 200 (Python random.sample, seed 22). For each project the script tested every route below. It used the BioProject accession, plus the SRP accession taken from one experiment's SRA XML. Every project had at least one SRA experiment.

Route Projects with a paper Share
BioProject XML Publication (PubMed ID or DOI) 8 4.0%
elink bioproject→pubmed 5 2.5%
elink sra→pubmed (up to 200 experiments) 6 3.0%
SRA STUDY_LINKS with a PubMed cross-reference 6 3.0%
PubMed databank field, ACC[si] 6 3.0%
Any of the five NCBI routes above 15 7.5%
Europe PMC ACCESSION_ID: (text-mined accession) 62 31%
Europe PMC plain search for the accession 98 49%
ENA study PubMed cross-reference 2 1.0%
Any route 102 51%

All counts are measured. What they show:

  • The BioProject Publication element feeds the Entrez links. The 5 elink hits are exactly the 5 projects whose Publication holds a PubMed ID. The other 3 hold only a DOI and get no link. The sra→pubmed link and the SRA STUDY_LINKS agree with each other in all 6 cases (5 overlap the BioProject ones). Measured overlap; the mechanism is inferred.
  • PubMed's databank field is a separate route. Its 6 hits share no project with the Publication route (measured). The publisher deposits the accession with the article, so the link lives on the PubMed record, not on the BioProject (inferred from the demo, where only the PubMed side records it).
  • Europe PMC finds the most, with some noise. Its plain search covers abstracts and open-access full text, so later papers that reuse a dataset also match. One glioblastoma project matched 12 articles. In a spot check of 20 projects, the earliest matching article was the data-generating paper in 18 or 19 (judged by title, topic and year; inferred). The ACCESSION_ID: field is narrower: it holds only accessions Europe PMC's text-mining tagged.
  • ENA adds nothing for NCBI-submitted projects: 2 hits, both already linked at NCBI (measured).

Caveats¶

  • One sample of 200; a 95% interval on 7.5% is roughly 4–11%, and on 49% roughly 42–56% (normal approximation, inferred).
  • The "raw sequence reads" set includes pathogen surveillance and genome-assembly read sets. These seldom have a dedicated paper, which pulls the shares down (inferred).
  • A Europe PMC hit is a citation of the accession, not a declared link. Taking the earliest article as "the paper" is a heuristic, and articles without open full text are missed (inferred).
  • Europe PMC and ENA are non-NCBI sources; they are used here only to measure what the NCBI records miss.
